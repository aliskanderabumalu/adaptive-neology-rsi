Use Case

When to invoke

A term is being proposed, coined, renamed, or imported into a shared reasoning environment.

What it filters

Decorative jargon, duplicate analogues, vague abstractions, and terms that increase coordination cost.

What it preserves

Useful conceptual compression, missing distinctions, agent self-correction, and testable cognitive handles.

Hard Failure Rule

FAIL

If a proposed term has an identical existing analogue, it fails. The reviewer may recommend the existing term or revise the proposal into a narrower distinction, but the duplicate neologism is not accepted.
