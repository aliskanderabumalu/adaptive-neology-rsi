# Lexicon Governance

The lexicon is governed by usefulness, not novelty.

## Roles

- **Proposer:** drafts a term and supplies examples.
- **Reviewer:** scores the term with the rubric.
- **Maintainer:** moves entries between proposal, accepted, rejected, and retired states.
- **Agent:** may propose, critique, or apply terms, but must expose uncertainty.

## Acceptance Rules

A term may be accepted when:

- the entry is complete;
- the rubric score is at least 16/25;
- clarity, utility, and misuse control are not zero;
- at least one reviewer can apply the term to a new example;
- the term has a retirement condition.

## Rejection Rules

Reject a term when it:

- has an identical existing analogue;
- is only a synonym;
- cannot be applied to a concrete case;
- increases ambiguity;
- creates prestige language;
- duplicates an accepted entry.

## Pruning

Every accepted term should be periodically reviewed.

Terms may be:

- kept;
- revised;
- merged;
- deprecated;
- retired.

## Community Rule

The community may be imaginative, but the lexicon must remain lean.

Expansion is allowed only when it increases usable cognition.
